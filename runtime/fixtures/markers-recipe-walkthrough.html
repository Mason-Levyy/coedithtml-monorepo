Weeknight Lentil Soup

A 30-minute soup that uses one pot and pantry staples.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups red lentils
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 tbsp cumin

Steps

  1. Sauté the onion and carrots until soft.
  2. Add lentils, cumin, and 6 cups water.
  3. Simmer 20 minutes, then blend half the soup.
