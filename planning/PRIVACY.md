## 1. What information do we collect?

### Information you provide to us

**In short: we collect the files you upload, the comments and edits you make,
and any name you choose to give yourself.**

**Uploaded artifacts.** When you upload an HTML file, we store that file and
serve it to anyone holding a valid link. We store it exactly as you provided it.
See Section 2, which is the most important section of this notice.

**Overlay content.** Comments, replies, sticky notes, and edits you make to a
shared artifact are stored separately from the artifact itself and associated
with the link they were made on.

**Self-declared identity.** You may enter a display name so other participants
can tell who wrote what. We do not verify it. It is stored with your comments
and visible to everyone with access to that link.

**Contact information.** If you email us, subscribe to updates, or opt in to
comment notifications, we collect the email address you provide.

**We do not charge for the Services.** There are no paid plans, and we do not
collect payment information of any kind.

### Information collected automatically

**In short: standard server and diagnostic data.**

We automatically collect certain information when you use the Services. This
does not by itself reveal your identity, but may include your IP address,
browser and device characteristics, operating system, language preferences,
referring URL, approximate country-level location derived from IP address, and
information about how and when you use the Services.

**Log and usage data.** Our servers record request logs including timestamps,
pages and artifacts accessed, actions taken, and error reports. This is used to
operate the Services, prevent abuse, and diagnose faults.

We do not collect precise geolocation data, and we do not use GPS or device
location APIs.

## 2. Artifacts and the content you upload

**In short: we store and serve your files, we do not inspect them, and you are
responsible for what is inside them.**

This section describes the core of the Services and deserves particular
attention.

**We do not review uploaded artifacts.** Files are stored and served
automatically. We do not read, analyze, index, or moderate their contents except
where necessary to operate the Services, respond to a legal obligation, or
investigate a report of abuse.

**Your uploads may contain other people's personal information.** An artifact
may include names, financial figures, customer records, or confidential business
material. Where that is the case, you are the controller of that information and
we act as a processor on your instructions. **You are responsible for having the
right to upload that content and for complying with any confidentiality
obligation, employment agreement, or client contract that applies to it.**

**Do not upload material you are not permitted to share.** If your employer or
client restricts where their information may be stored, do not upload it here.

**Anyone with the link can access the artifact.** Links are unguessable, but
they are not access-controlled unless you set a password. Anyone who receives a
link — including someone it was forwarded to — can view the artifact, and if
they hold an edit link, modify it. Treat a link as the credential it is.

**Artifacts are served from a separate domain** from the Website. This is a
security measure: it prevents an uploaded file from accessing your session on
our Website.

**Deletion.** Artifacts are automatically deleted after 30 days without access,
along with all comments and edits associated with them. You may also request
earlier deletion at any time by emailing privacy@coedithtml.com with the link to
the artifact. Deletion removes the artifact from active systems; residual copies
may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

Because there are no accounts, the link is the only identifier we hold for an
artifact. Keep a copy of any link you may want to manage later — without it we
cannot locate your artifact in order to delete it.

learning models, and we do not sell them.

## 6. Do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

**In short: only what is needed to make the product work.**

We use cookies and local browser storage for essential functions: keeping you
signed in to your own dashboard, remembering the display name you chose for a
link, and retaining your reading position and view preferences.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not permit third parties to place

set by an artifact you open is set by that artifact, not by us — see Section 7.

Most browsers accept cookies by default and can be configured to reject them.
Rejecting essential cookies will prevent parts of the Services from working.

## 7. Third-party code inside artifacts

**In short: artifacts can run their own code, and we do not control what it
does.**

Artifacts are HTML documents that may contain their own JavaScript, stylesheets,
fonts, images, and network requests. When you open a shared artifact, that code
runs in your browser.

An artifact may load resources from, or transmit information to, servers we do
not operate and have not vetted. We isolate artifacts on a separate domain so
they cannot access your session on our Website, but we cannot prevent an
artifact from communicating with a third party of its own.

**Open artifacts only from people you trust.** If you are concerned about the
behavior of a specific artifact, report it to us at abuse@coedithtml.com.

## 9. How long do we keep your information?

**In short: for as long as your artifact exists, plus a short window.**

- **Artifacts and overlay content** are automatically deleted after 30 days
  without access, or earlier on request. Any access to the link — by you or by
  anyone you shared it with — restarts the 30-day period.
- **Server logs** are retained for 90 days.
- **Support and privacy correspondence** is retained for 24 months.
- **Backups** are overwritten on a rolling 30 day cycle.

When we no longer have a legitimate business need to process your information,
we will delete or anonymize it, or, where that is not immediately possible,
securely store it and isolate it from further processing until deletion is
possible.

## 10. How do we keep your information safe?

**In short: reasonable technical and organizational measures, and no absolute
guarantee.**

We use encryption in transit, encryption at rest for stored artifacts,
unguessable link tokens, origin isolation for untrusted content, and access
controls limiting who on our side can reach production systems.

However, no transmission over the Internet and no storage technology is entirely
secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized third parties will never defeat
our safeguards. Transmission of information to and from the Services is at your
own risk, and you should only access the Services in a secure environment.

**Please read Section 2 again regarding confidential material.** The most likely
cause of unwanted disclosure is not a technical breach but a link forwarded to
someone it was not intended for.

coeditHTML is operated by one individual, not a company with a dedicated
security team. We take the measures described above seriously, but you should
weigh that fact when deciding what to upload.

## 13. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT")
feature. No uniform technology standard for recognizing DNT signals has been
finalized. We do not currently respond to DNT browser signals. We also do not
engage in the cross-site behavioral tracking that DNT was designed to limit. If
a standard is adopted that we must follow, we will update this notice.
