Step 3 of 5 · View

View mode is interactive and read-only.

Share a view link when your document is ready to present. Viewers can explore dashboards, decks, and interactive prototypes on any device without installing software or creating an account.

The original content is protected against accidental edits.

Step 4 of 5 · Suggest

Suggest mode lets collaborators leave feedback.

Two sample notes are already waiting on this page. Sticky notes can be placed anywhere (including charts and images), while comments attach to highlighted sentences. All feedback syncs in real time to the sidebar.

A review usually runs in the same order every time: first you upload the file, then you send the link, then you read what came back.

Try it out: reply to both notes, then select any sentence to leave a comment of your own.
