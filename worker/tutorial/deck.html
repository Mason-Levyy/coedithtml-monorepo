Step 2 of 5 · Sharing

Three modes, one link.

You upload the file once. Then you pick which of these three modes the person on the other end gets. Click one to see what it does.

They open the link and read the page. That is all. No notes, no edits, no account, and no way to change a thing you sent.

They can stick notes anywhere on the page and comment on any sentence, without altering a word of the file itself. You read it all back in one place and decide what to keep.

In most cases, this is the one to send.

They can rewrite the text in place, right where it sits on the page. The words change. The layout, the styling, and the code underneath stay exactly as they were.

Step 3 of 5 · View

A view link is just a look.

Hand someone a view link and the page arrives as a finished thing. They can read it on a phone, on a laptop, in bed, without installing anything or signing into anything.

Nothing they do can change it. Send this one when the work is done and you only want it seen.

Step 4 of 5 · Suggest

A suggest link lets people answer.

Two notes are already waiting on this page. A sticky lands anywhere, even on a picture with no words to select. A comment attaches to a sentence you highlight. Both carry the name of whoever wrote them, and both show up in the panel on the right.

A review usually runs in the same order every time: first you upload the file, then you send the link, then you read what came back.

Do this: reply to both notes, then select a sentence of your own and leave a third.
