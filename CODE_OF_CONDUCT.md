# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behavior that helps: being respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and
accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the
project and the people using it.

Behavior that does not: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, harassment in public or
private, publishing others' private information without permission, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **team@coedithtml.com**. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and
security will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will
explain the reason when they do. Sustained or severe violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban from participation.
